About

Founded by Aishwarya Arun, an accomplished dancer with an M.A. in Mohiniyattam from Mahatma Gandhi University, Sapthara School of Dance celebrates the timeless beauty of classical Indian dance through Bharatanatyam and Mohiniyattam.

Our classes blend discipline, grace, and creativity, helping students of all ages evolve from basics to confident stage performers.

Classes & Admission

Bharatanatyam

A captivating South Indian classical dance that blends rhythm, storytelling, and expressive grace. Suitable for learners aged 4 and above.

Mohiniyattam

Kerala’s dance of grace and emotion, known for its fluidity and charm. Designed for students aged 4 and above.

Admissions are open year-round through a simple, welcoming process.
